# MAE 3 ROBOT TEAM 32

## Project Description
In Spring 2025, students were assigned the tasks of designing a remote controlled robot that had the ability to place 8g rings on a miniature statue at various heights. My team used a rack and pinion system giving us 2 DOF and a direct drive DriveTrain system enabling movement across the stadium. 

## Materials Used
- Acrylic
- Delrin
- Rubber O-rings
- PLA (3D Printed)
- Aluminum Angles
- Geared Motors (Provided) 

## Softwares and Machinery used for Manufacturing 
- FUSION 360
- AUTOCAD
- Laser Cutter
- Drill Press
- Sheet Metal Bender
- Hand Saw 

## Issues and Solutions 
- During motion, rack had lots of wobble and stability issues. Fixed with Aluminum rods that slide through the slider of the rack.
- Acrylic does not have a lot of friction which was seen with the wheels slipping on the acrylic base. This was addressed with a 3 piece wheel design that allowed for the fitting of a rubber o-ring which has much more friction against an acrylic base.
- It was very hard to be precise with alignment with the columns connected to the rack and pinion setup. The use of a custom 3D printed u-bracket proved to have less room for error along with 3D printed spacers that were placed along the aluminum rods that constrained the rack's movement. 

## Results 
Our robot was able to score 1240 points and averaged 930 points placing us in the higher percentile of competitors allowing us to clear the first two rounds of the tournament.
